
Kratikos Labs • Project Guidelines

Thank you for your interest in our Asynchronous Agentic Workflows framework. Because this repository currently serves as an abstract architectural specification, we are focused on structural validation and conceptual rigor rather than active codebase implementations.

How to Contribute:
We welcome high-level technical feedback and analytical insights through Issues and Discussions. Specifically, we are looking for contributions in the following areas:

Edge-Case Analysis: Identifying real-world scenarios or high-risk operational domains where the proposed asynchronous orchestration pattern might face unique bottlenecks or strain.
Deterministic Guardrail Review: Offering feedback on the boundaries, schema validation structures, and circuit-breaker thresholds defined in our specification.
Staged Autonomy Progression Feedback: Providing input on the empirical metrics and stage gates used to transition systems from human-in-the-loop to autonomous execution.

Submission Guidelines:
Open an Issue: For architectural critiques or structural edge-cases, please open a detailed Issue outlining the specific domain, the perceived risk, and your proposed mitigation.
Keep it Abstract: Please ensure all discussions and submissions remain focused on the universal enterprise design pattern rather than vendor-specific implementations.
As the framework evolves into an open reference implementation, these guidelines will expand to accommodate active code pull requests.
